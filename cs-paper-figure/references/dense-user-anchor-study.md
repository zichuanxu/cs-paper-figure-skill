# The two user-selected complexity benchmarks

These are figure-level observations from the actual PDFs and attachments. Measured source sizes describe the source placement; they are not production recommendations.

## IdeaScientist: Orchestrating Agents for Grounded Scientific Ideation

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.04074v1 · v1 · Meta / FAIR

PDF page 1 lists Meta Reality Labs as affiliation 2 and FAIR at Meta as affiliation 3; university collaborators are retained. The PDF header date October 6 differs from arXiv first submission October 2; use arXiv date for first publication.

### Figure 1: Overview, role specifications, empirical summary and worked case

Page 2; crop [96.0, 65.0, 520.0, 503.0] PDF points.

**Composition.** Portrait composite: about three fifths of height is the system and knowledge vault; the lower band puts a narrow performance panel beside a wider worked case. A vault table sits above an orchestrator and three aligned role cards. Each role repeats Input / Action / Output. Dusty blue title tabs interrupt light dashed rounded scopes.

**Information levels.** research system; role input/action/output contracts; structured knowledge representation; performance summary; concrete causal-test/training case.

**Concrete devices.** stacked four-column vault table; three aligned role mini-tables; domain-distribution pie; small robot and document icons; hatched horizontal bars with values; dialogue snippets; before/after miniature charts.

**Density and critique.** High semantic density with three connected readings: system, benefit, and instance. Many labels are about 5.6 pt at the actual source placement and several numeric details are smaller. Much explanatory text uses gray, and the narrow performance legend needs enlargement. Strong complexity does not make every typographic choice a good production default.

**Routing.** Orchestrator dispatches downward to three role modules; heavy gray arrows connect the roles and lower supporting agents. Independent performance and worked-case regions are separated by dashed scope borders.

**Palette.** headings / primary blue: #5E7D94 at alpha 1.00; deep evidence ink: #14293D at alpha 1.00; warm paper surface: #FAF8F3 at alpha 1.00; gap-finder surface: #EAF2F6 at alpha 1.00; innovator surface: #EAF4EF at alpha 1.00; writer surface: #F7EEDF at alpha 1.00; deadline emphasis: #E0A43A at alpha 1.00.

**Visible font evidence.** TimesNewRomanPSMT 5.60 pt; TimesNewRomanPSMT 5.61 pt; TimesNewRomanPSMT 4.86 pt; TimesNewRomanPS-BoldMT 7.38 pt; TimesNewRomanPS-BoldMT 6.49 pt; TimesNewRomanPSMT 6.49 pt; TimesNewRomanPSMT 6.50 pt; TimesNewRomanPSMT 7.38 pt; TimesNewRomanPS-BoldMT 7.37 pt; TimesNewRomanPS-BoldMT 6.50 pt.

**Transferable decisions.**

- Combine an overview with a concrete instance of the same mechanism, not an unrelated decorative vignette.
- Use a repeated role schema to compare agents without rereading paragraphs.
- Allocate the worked case more width than the compact empirical summary.
- Repeat role identity through headers and artifacts, while keeping body text dark enough to read.

**Do not copy.**

- Do not reproduce the small pale text or shrink a full-page composite to one column.
- Do not copy the domain pie unless its composition matters to the scientific argument.
- Do not treat short bar summaries as a substitute for uncertainty and full evaluation context.

### Figure 2: Domain profile paired with a human-comparison interval plot

Page 8; crop [78.0, 61.0, 548.0, 235.0] PDF points.

**Composition.** Two side-by-side panels: a multi-system domain radar with a long legend at left, and directly annotated orange points with horizontal confidence intervals at right. A dotted 50% reference line distinguishes chance/equality in the right panel. Domain sample sizes sit next to radar labels.

**Information levels.** domain heterogeneity; pairwise human preference; sample-size and interval context.

**Concrete devices.** domain n labels; faint comparator radar lines; highlighted two-system profiles; direct point labels; horizontal bootstrap intervals; 50% reference.

**Density and critique.** The right panel communicates pairwise results efficiently. The radar creates a crowded legend and makes precise differences harder to compare; adapt its registered-domain structure as a dot plot or heatmap when exact comparison matters.

**Routing.** No computational edges. The radar polygon is a domain profile, whereas the right-side dots and intervals encode paired comparison results.

**Palette.** paired preference marks: #D55E00 at alpha 1.00; trained profile: #1F5FA8 at alpha 1.00; base profile: #B04A2F at alpha 1.00; reference grid: #D9D9D9 at alpha 1.00; comparator profile: #009E73 at alpha 0.82.

**Visible font evidence.** NimbusRoman-Regular 7.11 pt; NimbusRoman-Regular 7.11 pt; STIXGeneral-Regular 7.11 pt; STIXGeneral-Italic 7.11 pt; OptimisticDisp-Bd 7.01 pt; SFRM0800 7.97 pt.

**Transferable decisions.**

- Separate heterogeneity across domains from a paired head-to-head estimand.
- Put sample size near the domain identity and identify the confidence interval in the caption.
- Emphasize the principal systems and lighten comparators without making them unreadable.

**Do not copy.**

- Do not convert a radar-polygon area into an aggregate performance claim.
- Do not infer intervals for panels that do not show them.

## SkillOpt: Executive Strategy for Self-Evolving Agent Skills

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.23904v2 · v2 · Microsoft Research

PDF page 1 explicitly lists Microsoft as affiliation 1, alongside Shanghai Jiao Tong, Tongji, and Fudan.

### Figure 2: Parallel bounded editing with held-out gating and an epoch update

Page 4; crop [66.0, 58.0, 524.0, 316.0] PDF points.

**Composition.** A wide upper pipeline runs from train/validation/test split cards through rollout evidence, three representative minibatch/optimizer/edit lanes, vertical merge, budget, modifier, and gate. The lower teal region expands epoch-wise replay and reflection. Main flow uses compact serif labels, document glyphs, an amber diamond, and strong role colors.

**Information levels.** dataset access boundaries; parallel batch computation; atomic textual edits; strict validation acceptance and rejection; slow epoch/meta update; deployment state.

**Concrete devices.** locked test card; frozen-agent lock; folded skill documents; miniature scored-trajectory plots; ADD/DEL/REP operation chips; rejected-edit buffer; four replay outcome categories; optimizer meta-skill stack.

**Density and critique.** The architecture is substantially richer than a simple flowchart. At its source placement, common main labels are about 6.16 pt, minibatch details about 3.70 pt, and some glyph labels about 2.46 pt. A full-size recreation should expand the view or split the operator detail rather than copy the microtype. Distinguish all mutable states and the intended destination of each return route.

**Routing.** Left-to-right minibatch branches merge before a validation gate. Accepted state is blue; red dashed rejection returns to the merge. A separate teal dashed epoch region returns a slow update and optimizer-context update. The final teal return appears to end at the current-skill document even though its label names future optimizer calls; reproduce the intended dependency only after checking the method text.

**Palette.** optimizer family: #CAB2D6 at alpha 1.00; optimizer outline: #67317D at alpha 1.00; skill document: #B2CEEC at alpha 1.00; skill outline: #2D5D9D at alpha 1.00; training / improvement: #ADD797 at alpha 1.00; epoch update: #0A7F81 at alpha 1.00; neutral artifact: #EDEEEF at alpha 1.00.

**Visible font evidence.** TimesNewRomanPSMT 6.16 pt; TimesNewRomanPSMT 3.70 pt; TimesNewRomanPS-ItalicMT 6.16 pt; TimesNewRomanPS-BoldMT 6.16 pt; TimesNewRomanPSMT 4.93 pt; TimesNewRomanPS-ItalicMT 4.93 pt; TimesNewRomanPS-BoldMT 7.39 pt; TimesNewRomanPSMT 2.46 pt; TimesNewRomanPS-ItalicMT 3.70 pt; TimesNewRomanPS-ItalicMT 2.46 pt.

**Transferable decisions.**

- Draw training evidence, validation selection, and final testing as separate access rules.
- Make the atomic operation visible with an example edit, not only an optimizer box.
- Use a separate spatial band for the slower timescale and identify what it actually updates.
- Show the rejected path and a bounded candidate, not only successful forward execution.

**Do not copy.**

- Do not claim a frozen model has no mutable external state.
- Do not copy ambiguous return-arrow placement; route to the exact optimizer context or skill state.
- Do not reproduce 2–4 pt labels or icons whose details vanish at manuscript size.

### Figure 3: Train, validation-selected and test checkpoint trajectories

Page 12; crop [65.0, 59.0, 520.0, 246.0] PDF points.

**Composition.** Three equal-width benchmark panels share a legend above. Blue circles/solid lines denote train rollout, orange squares/dashes selection-best, and green triangles/dash-dot unseen test. Only light horizontal grid rules are used. Epoch labels are equal-spaced checkpoint categories, not a linear numeric time axis; benchmark y ranges differ.

**Information levels.** within-benchmark learning trajectory; selection-versus-test behavior; cross-task generalization limits.

**Concrete devices.** shared role legend; redundant line and marker identities; checkpoint categories; small multiples with explicit y ticks.

**Density and critique.** Economical analytical companion to the dense method figure. The differing y scales reveal small within-benchmark changes but prevent visually comparing slopes across panels. The result is useful partly because one benchmark exposes a selection–test gap rather than flattening it into an aggregate.

**Routing.** Connected checkpoint values are within-benchmark trajectories. Line dash and marker shape redundantly distinguish train, validation-selected and test series.

**Palette.** training: #0072B2 at alpha 1.00; validation selection: #D55E00 at alpha 1.00; unseen test: #009E73 at alpha 1.00; grid: #D0D0D0 at alpha 0.55.

**Visible font evidence.** DejaVuSans 6.41 pt; DejaVuSans 6.98 pt; DejaVuSans 7.80 pt; DejaVuSans 6.57 pt.

**Transferable decisions.**

- Keep data-split roles fixed across all panels and distinguish them by both marker and line style.
- Use companion plots to reveal behavior hidden by a method overview.
- State checkpoint spacing and per-panel axis limits explicitly.

**Do not copy.**

- Do not read categorical checkpoint spacing as linear elapsed training.
- Do not compare slopes visually across unmatched y scales.
- Do not describe a test trace as a selection criterion.
