# Trending-paper study, 2026-11: 29 papers that readers chose to share

The venue study read figures that committees rewarded. This one reads figures that readers picked out:
two paper-reading feeds on Xiaohongshu each showed one figure per paper for the papers and reports of the
first half of November 2026, and one more paper was added by hand. Almost all are arXiv preprints from
AI labs and universities on agents, benchmarks, language-model systems and representation learning; one is
a Nature Communications manuscript and one a designed long-form essay.

About 270 figures were opened as crops from the PDFs (the 28 featured ones and every other figure of the
main bodies, plus the more designed appendix figures) and measured: printed width, text sizes from live
text, stroke widths from the drawing commands, white share from renders. The rules that came out are in
[visual-craft.md](visual-craft.md) and [registers.md](registers.md) (§8 "What gets a figure chosen", §9
evaluation figures); this file is the evidence.

Read it when one of those rules looks wrong for the figure in hand, or when you want a published model for
a family. Being shared does not make a figure good: about a third of the featured figures appear among the
counter-examples below.

## What was measured

| Quantity | Strong figures | Weak figures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Width | 140–143 mm (NeurIPS-style block) or 163–172 mm (report block); column 77–84 mm; margin figures 38–59 mm | The same widths; a 198 mm figure runs off the page |
| Height of chart figures | 37–80 mm at full width | 130–190 mm for two simple panels |
| Text sizes | 8.0 pt only (FrontierPhysics); 6.4 and 7.7 (JetSpec++); 7 in schematics and 9 in charts (Neural Thickets); 7–10.5 (LeanPRM); 8–10 in charts (Accelerate Bench, IdeaTaste) | 9 to 22 sizes in one figure; minimum 1.4–3.5 pt |
| Share of characters under 5.8 pt | 0–8 % | 44 % (Persona 1), 49 % at 4.5 pt (LoopKV 1), 81 % (CoCA 2), 100 % under 5 pt in nine of fifteen figures (Harness) |
| Structural strokes | One or two weights: 0.5 and 0.74 pt (FrontierPhysics), 0.51–0.64 (JetSpec++), 0.47 / 1.17 / 1.94 for box, arrow and spine (Neural Thickets 3) | Eight widths from 0.33 to 0.80 pt (Persona 1); 0.26–0.53 hairlines (ToolSpace 2) |
| Chart lines | 0.83–1.2 pt for all series; ours 1.19 pt against 0.83 among ten curves (H-PDLP 2) | 0.31 pt band edges; 1.0 pt lines under five overlapping CI bands |
| Hues | One accent on neutrals (LeanPRM); two (IdeaTaste 1); three role classes (Neural Thickets 3); three families with lightness steps for six groups (Self-Evolving) | 7 to 13 in one figure; tab20; pure RGB |
| White share | Featured schematics 45–72 %, their papers' charts 80–92 % | 13.6 % (SkillMem 3), 19 % (prompt cards), 24 % (grey ggplot theme) |
| Rasters | None, or logos and real data only | Whole figures at 230–410 dpi; text baked in |

Further measurements the rulebook cites:

- **Scaling, not style, makes type small.** In *How Much of a Harness…* nine of fifteen figures have every
  character under 5 pt at 168.7 mm (median 4.4 pt); the same authors' 127 mm Fig. 8 is at 6.6–7.2 pt.
  The same holds for ServeLearnBench Fig. 3 (61 mm, 4.1–5.5 pt) and SkillMem Fig. 1 (127 mm, 4.3–5.2 pt).
- **Chart beside schematic.** In both papers that put a full chart beside a schematic in one figure (CoCA
  1, Parallel LMs 1) the chart's text fell to 3.5–4.5 pt. A result chart alone on page 1 stayed at 8.0 pt
  (FrontierPhysics 1) and 5.5–8 pt (Long Context 1).
- **Rotated names.** Twenty-two model names at 45° take about a third of the height of FrontierPhysics
  Fig. 1.
- **What was featured.** All 27 featured paper figures are their paper's Figure 1 or 2; the essay's is a
  set of neuron cards from page 19. About a third are result charts with no diagram. Neither of the two
  image-generator paintings in the set (TreeOPSD 1, the essay's title art) was the figure a feed chose.

## What the study confirms

Every weak figure here breaks a rule the skill already had, which is the main finding: the rulebook's
budgets separate the strong figures of this set from the weak ones.

- Labels at 5.8 pt or above: the strong papers pass, every weak figure fails.
- Two stroke weights, two or three text sizes: JetSpec++ (two sizes) against Persona 1 (18 sizes, 8
  widths), SkillMem 3 (19 sizes), Accelerate Bench 1 (22 sizes).
- Repeat one drawing and change one thing: JetSpec++ 1 a/b, LoopKV 6, Diffusion 1A, Neural Thickets 3/7/8,
  Sleep 8, Work Agents 2.
- One concrete example through every stage: LeanPRM 1, JetSpec++ 4, Parallel LMs 2 (the paper's own title
  as the input tokens), Aligning Autoresearch 7.
- Colour is a name: held by Neural Thickets, JetSpec++, Self-Evolving Agents, Agent Plasticity, LeanPRM;
  broken by Harness (two systems swap colours between Figs. 4 and 11), Parallel LMs (orange means four
  things), MASS, SkillMem.
- The typeset table and the islands: MASS 3 is a chart and three tabulars under one number; ToolSpace 4 is
  six islands at 3.0–4.5 pt; TEM 5, 7 and 9 are photos, a chart and a table sharing nothing.
- Bare charts: the tinted, framed, gridded axes of Harness and Muse Glimmer are the weakest charts.
- Radar: Muse Glimmer 2 passes "at most four polygons" and fails everything else.

## What it adds or changes

| Skill before | What these figures do | Rule now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| At most four hues | Six groups as three hue families with lightness steps (Self-Evolving); eight identity colours kept across 40 figures (Agent Plasticity); vendor hue with the logo as marker (FrontierPhysics) | The budget counts families and roles; identity colours are allowed paper-wide and never excuse a schematic (visual-craft §3) |
| Colour is a name | Two kinds of thing reuse one palette and collide | Two kinds, two disjoint palettes; a hue never changes owner (§3) |
| Three text sizes | One size for a whole paper, hierarchy by weight and grey; sizes half a point apart read as one | Count size roles; one size can carry a paper (§4) |
| No cards, no frames | Filled stage cards work when each is one real unit holding its artefact (LeanPRM 1, Neural Thickets 1); nested cards destroy the type size | A container is a region something enters or leaves; one level; never a whole panel (§7) |
| Header bands nowhere | Dark title bars on real files and terminals read as the object | Window chrome belongs to windows (§7) |
| Tints mean scope | A fully tinted task folder answers "who sees this file" (FrontierPhysics 3, 9 % white) | A full tint may answer one ownership question, keyed in one line beneath (§3) |
| Ours has the same line weight | With ten curves the equal-weight rule hides ours (H-PDLP 2: 1.19 against 0.83 pt) | Family hue plus dash for members; ours may be the one heavier line (§11) |
| Labels of one to four words | Claim sentences as panel heads in the best multi-panel charts | A panel head may state its finding (§10) |
| Method overview is a plate with trays | Five different drawings on a through-line with a counts strip, no container (FrontierPhysics 2) | Trays may go when every stage is a drawn object (registers §6) |
| Idea beside its measurement | Value printed in each component (Neural Thickets 3); three bars at the pipeline's exit (LoopKV 1) | The schematic can be the result chart; a method figure may end on the number (§1) |
| "No arrows where reading order will do" | Verdicts aligned to the rows they judge; the absent edge drawn and struck | Aligned rows, `cv.no_flow`, `cv.spring` (§9) |
| Leaderboard = sorted bars | Sorted bars claim an order single runs do not establish | Show intervals or bracket the unseparated rows (registers §9) |
| Charts: claim chart or field chart | Row-aligned measure panels, table-charts, frontier scatters, concept plots, dossiers | Registers §9, with two models |

## Flaws in shared figures, not to copy

- **Type built large and scaled down**: Harness 2–7, 9–15; ServeLearnBench 3, 5; SkillMem 1; Muse Glimmer
  1–3; TEM 3; MolRSI 2b (110 labels at 1.4 pt in a sunburst).
- **Slide-kit plates**: CoCA 2 (outer frame, six dashed frames in five colours, emoji, seven hues, four
  arrow styles), ToolSpace 2 (gradient header bands, eight dashed frames, a robot mascot, 11 sizes),
  SkillMem 2 and 3 (gradient discs and panels; eight boxed equations as a figure), LoopKV 1 (four levels
  of nesting, "Contribution N" heads, prose in cards).
- **Generated art that replaces nothing**: Work Agents 1, 5, 7, 11 (rendered robot, toolbox and planets
  beside line icons), TreeOPSD 1 (an unlabelled painting as Figure 1), a cartoon scientist four times in
  MolRSI 1, a robot mascot in ServeLearnBench 2, a stock brain in Sleep 1.
- **Boxed prose and placeholders**: Sleep 1 (about 130 words in four boxes), MASS 1 ("role: … instruction:
  …"), Work Agents 8 (a tree of citation numbers), MolRSI S1 and S2.
- **Radar and ring**: Muse Glimmer 2 (22 axes, two inverted, three pure-RGB series, a 230 dpi bitmap);
  Accelerate Bench 2 (a ring of bars: upside-down labels, 13 legend entries, no comparison across sectors).
- **Colour**: pure primaries (Diffusion 1–7; three translucent bands overprint to brown in Fig. 3); a
  rainbow map for an ordered variable (Diffusion 4); eight meaningless hues on eight bars (Aligning
  Autoresearch 5); tab20 with 13 bands (Harness 6, 12–15); ours in yellow (Sleep 6).
- **Axes**: bars from 60, 75 or 80 % (Agent Plasticity 6, AudioFM 5a, ServeLearnBench 6); second y axes
  (ServeLearnBench 4, 12); data cut by the y limit (ServeLearnBench 5); three panels with three silent
  scales (Long Context 1); two delta panels with different x scales drawn alike (SkillMem 1).
- **Legends and labels**: a boxed legend per panel or over the data (TEM 3, Harness 1, Sleep 3–5, MASS 7);
  unlabelled reference lines (LoopKV 12, MolRSI S4); a legend beside direct labels with collisions (LoopKV
  9, 10); five hatch patterns over seven fills (LoopKV 5).
- **The handwriting face**: Accelerate Bench 1 sets a strong, concrete comparison (real files, real
  diffs, real tables) in Comic Sans with code at 2.3–5 pt.
- **Interactive residue** in a printed essay: hover hints, pagers, sliders and tab buttons (Goodfire).
- **Screens**: a dark screenshot of a web demo at 5 % white (ToolSpace 3).

## What two fresh runs of the skill showed

After the rules above were written, two fresh agents drew three figures from one-paragraph briefs with
the skill alone: a timing diagram, a leaderboard with a human reference row, and a cost plot. All three
used the new families unprompted. The timing diagram is now the model for its family
([`scripts/examples/timing/render_speculation.py`](../scripts/examples/timing/render_speculation.py)).
Their reports changed the skill in these places: a shorter reading path for charts; the family entry
wins over its register's general bullets; a stated total that the stated parts cannot give is drawn as
derived and reported; the cold reader's findings are checked against the render; a reference row and
family hues for a benchmark with no system of its own; chart labels are paired in `text_overlaps`.
